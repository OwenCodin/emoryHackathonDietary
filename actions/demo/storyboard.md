# Demo storyboard

Generated footage sets context; real recordings show the working product.
Obtain consent before recording any real person or FaceTime call. Use synthetic
patient details and do not display credentials, personal health information,
or payment screens.

| Shot | Source | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Patient at a kitchen table with diet handouts | Generated | Establish the daily burden; do not show readable medical records. |
| Title card | Edited graphic | Product name and "FaceTime your renal dietitian." |
| Label held up during the call | Real screen recording | Show the live agent's actual spoken response. |
| Grocery cart filling during the call | Real screen recording | Show real browser activity; stop before checkout. |
| Mealtime reminder | Real screen recording | Show the actual call or message received with consent. |
| Phone pointed at a store shelf | Real footage | Film in a store with permission; do not include bystanders. |
| Finished meal | Generated | Warm closing image; do not imply medical advice or a guaranteed outcome. |
| Closing card | Edited graphic | Team and project information. |

Do not use generated footage to depict the product operating. Do not record
until the relevant flow works and everyone on the call has consented.
