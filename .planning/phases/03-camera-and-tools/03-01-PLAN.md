This plan builds the camera watcher, wires it into the call, adds the `look_at_camera` and `check_food` tools with safe results and verdict wording, updates the instructions, and teaches the simulated caller to show a picture and speak, then proves all three paths on simulated calls.

**As a** kidney or dialysis patient on a FaceTime call, **I want to** show or name a food and hear a short verdict based on my own limits, **so that** I know whether I can eat it, and the agent never guesses when it cannot see.
