## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim; not in user-story format, see note in the planning return): The patient sees the shopping browser on the call while a cart fills, and a patient who answers an outbound call is greeted with a mealtime check-in.

This plan's slice: the caller sees the agent's picture, then the browser (a magenta stand-in) while a cart runs, then the agent's picture again, proven on a simulated call.

<objective>
The agent's video track shows Track C's browser frames while a cart is running and the agent's own picture otherwise, without a per-pixel redraw of the whole face on every tick. The simulated caller proves the switch (face, then cart, then face) against a running agent that uses a built-in fake cart provider, with no phone and without Track C's module.

Purpose: VID-01 and VID-02 (the patient sees the agent, then the browser during a cart run, then the agent again).
Output: agent/video.mjs, cached face frames in agent/face.mjs, agent/sim/fake-cart.mjs, sim --expect-cart-switch, wiring in agent/agent.mjs, unit tests, and one simulated proof.
</objective>
