## Phase Goal

**As a** kidney or dialysis patient, **I want to** FaceTime the line, be greeted in time by an AI renal dietitian who tells me it follows my care team's limits, and talk with it, on this call and the next one, **so that** I can get food guidance by simply making a FaceTime call.

(The ROADMAP goal line for Phase 2 is "A patient can FaceTime the line, is greeted in time, and holds a spoken conversation with the dietitian persona, repeatably". The three slots above restate that line plus CALL-04 and add nothing else.)
