## Phase Goal

**As a** patient whose store call is cut off at five minutes, **I want to** be warned before the call ends and find my shopping list still there when I call back, **so that** I can carry on shopping where I stopped.

<objective>
A shopping list and the approved and rejected items survive a hangup, because each call is a separate process and the list is kept in a small per-patient file (D-04, D-05). The agent manages the list with three tools (D-06). A call back greets with "I remember your list" (D-07). About four minutes in, the agent warns that the call will end and asks for a call back (D-08). Proven offline (D-10) and on simulated calls (D-11, D-12).

Purpose: the trial ends calls at 5 minutes, so shopping in a store needs to carry on across calls.
Output: agent/memory.mjs and its tests, three list tools, the resume greeting and the time warning in the call, sim-call speech segments, .env.example entries, and the D-11 and D-12 proof in the SUMMARY.
</objective>
